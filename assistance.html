Internet Down?

Try These Quick Steps First

  • Restart your modem and router. Unplug for 30 seconds, plug the modem in first, wait for the lights, then the router.
  • Check cables. Make sure coax, Ethernet and power cables are snug.
  • Test another device. If only one device is affected, the issue may be that device or its Wi-Fi.
  • Check for an outage. Your provider's app, website or support line shows local outages.
  • Look at the lights. Red or blinking lights on the modem are worth reporting to your provider.

Important to know

We are an authorized sub-dealer, not your current provider. We cannot restore an outage, repair equipment or access your account. For active outages, contact your current provider directly.

Billing

Why Internet Bills Creep Up

Understanding the usual causes can help you compare plans fairly.

📅

Promotions Expire

Many plans start with a lower introductory price for a set period, then move to a standard rate.

🧾

Fees & Equipment Charges

Modem or router rental, taxes, regulatory fees and paper-bill fees can add to the monthly total.

📦

Bundles Change

Bundled TV, phone or mobile discounts can end or change, raising the combined price.

🏷️

Rate Increases

Providers can adjust prices over time. Comparing every so often helps you see where you stand.
