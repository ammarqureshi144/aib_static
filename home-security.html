How It Works

How a Monitored Security System Works

A simple chain from sensor to response.

01

Sensors Detect Activity

Door, window, motion, glass-break, smoke and other sensors watch for events around your home.

02

Control Panel & App Alert You

The system sounds an alarm if armed and sends an alert to your phone.

03

Monitoring Center Responds

With professional monitoring, trained agents receive the alert and try to reach you.

04

Emergency Services Contacted

If needed, the monitoring center can contact local emergency services. Response times vary.

Choosing the Right System

  • Apartment or condo: compact packages with door sensors, a doorbell camera and a hub.
  • Single-family home: more entry points, outdoor cameras and motion coverage.
  • Renters: look for self-install or no-drill equipment.
  • Smart home fans: ask about lights, locks and thermostat integration.
  • Pets: ask about pet-friendly motion sensors.

Questions to Ask Before You Buy

  • What is the monthly monitoring cost, and what does it include?
  • Is there a contract, and what are the cancellation terms?
  • Do I own the equipment or lease it?
  • Is there cellular or battery backup if power or internet goes out?
  • Are installation fees included or extra?
  • What happens to promotional pricing after the first term?
